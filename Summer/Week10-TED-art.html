Discussion Cards

Art, Aesthetics, and the Beauty of Daily Life

Work in trios. Pick 2–3 questions per card and dig into real opinions, not just quick answers. Click any highlighted word to check its meaning.
CARD 1

How We See Art

  • If you think about art from an anthropological angle — basically, what it says about being human — what do you think it reveals about us as a species?
  • Do you think there's an underlying unity in how humans across different cultures create art, even though the styles look nothing alike?
  • Do you have a favorite painting, building, sculpture, or even a clothing designer? What is it about their work that pulls you in?
  • Sometimes a piece of art has a hidden meaning that isn't clear at first glance. Has a painting or building ever surprised you once you learned the story behind it?
Key words
underlying unity hidden
CARD 2

Everyday Style: Colors and Patterns

  • How would you describe your everyday style? Do you gravitate toward bold colors and patterns, or do you keep things simple?
  • Do certain colors or patterns actually affect your mood, or is that just something people say?
  • If you had to weave together three words to describe your personal style, what would they be?
Key words
patterns weave together
CARD 3

Home, Seasons, and Little Details

  • Do you change your room's decorations with the seasons, the way a lot of Americans do — pumpkins in fall, flowers in spring?
  • Have you ever picked out something small, like tiles, a rug, or a vase, just because it made a space feel more "you"?
  • Do fresh flowers or small decorative touches actually change how you feel in a space, or is that just clever marketing?
Key word
tiles
CARD 4

Does Beauty Affect Motivation?

  • Do you think using nice stationery while studying, or eating from a nice plate, actually changes how motivated you feel — in spite of the fact that the task itself hasn't changed at all?
  • Do clean streets, green parks, and well-kept public buildings affect your mood when you're just walking through a city?
  • Do you regularly tidy up your desktop, wardrobe, or car, or does a little clutter not bother you at all?
Key word
in spite of
CARD 5

How Aesthetics Change Through Time

  • Style and taste unfold over decades and centuries — think about how different fashion or architecture looked just 100 years ago. What do you think really drives those changes?
  • Do you know of any style, trend, or even a word that was coined fairly recently but now feels like it's always existed?
  • Do you think taste in beauty looks completely different across continents, or are people more similar than we think?
Key words
unfold coined
CARD 6

The Big Takeaway

  • After everything we've talked about, do you think our mindset today is really that different from people's mindset 500 years ago, or do we just have different tools and technology?
  • If you had to give one takeaway from today's conversation about art, style, and daily life, what would it be?
  • Do you think future generations will look back at our current style and taste the same way we look back at the past — with curiosity, judgment, or admiration?
Key word
takeaway
