Warm-Up Speaking Cards

Growing Up, Coping, and What Success Really Means

Work in trios. Pick 2–3 questions from your card and discuss them out loud. Try to use at least two of the highlighted words in your answers — click any word (or the chips at the bottom) to check its meaning.
CARD 1

Upbringing: Strict or Relaxed?

  • Was your childhood steeped in rules, or did your parents let you make your own choices — even the wrong ones?
  • Would you describe your upbringing as more strict or more relaxed? Give a real example.
  • Looking back, do you think your parents handled discipline wisely? What would you do the same or differently?
Key words
steeped in upbringing wisely
CARD 2

Coping with Failure

  • Think of a time you really failed — a test, an interview, a plan. How did you cope with it?
  • Do you believe failure actually helps a person grow, or is it just something painful you have to survive?
  • What's the difference between someone who bounces back from failure and someone who gives up?
Key words
failure cope with growth
CARD 3

Talking About the Hard Stuff

  • When something painful happens — a breakup, losing a job — do you talk about it out loud, or keep it to yourself?
  • Has a breakup or a hard goodbye ever gripped you for a long time? How did you finally move on?
  • Do you use humor or wit to deal with hard situations, or does that feel fake to you?
Key words
to grip wit deeply
CARD 4

Entitled Behavior & Reality TV

  • Reality shows are full of people who act entitled, like the world owes them something. Do you see that in real life too?
  • Have you ever met someone luxuriating in privilege, who barely notices people who have less?
  • Would you ever quit a job or end a relationship on a whim, the way people do on TV?
Key words
entitled luxuriating on a whim
CARD 5

Rewards, Praise & Validation

  • How do you award yourself when you succeed at something?
  • How do you praise the people close to you when they do well?
  • Do we really need external validation — a prize, a compliment, likes on social media — or should success be enough on its own?
Key words
award praise validation
CARD 6

Private Schools vs. Public Schools

  • Have you noticed real differences between people who went to private schools and those who went to public schools?
  • Do you think kids from affluent families have an easier path to success just because of money, or is it more about attitude?
  • Instead of judging a school by its price, what should people actually look at?
Key words
affluent instead of waste
CARD 7

Can a No-Name University Still Get You There?

  • Can a small, no-name university still help someone succeed, or does the name of the school really matter?
  • Do some people cheat themselves out of good opportunities by only trusting famous universities?
  • Instead of focusing on rankings, should students focus more on personal growth?
Key words
cheat instead of growth
