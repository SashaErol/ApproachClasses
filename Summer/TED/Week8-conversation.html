Conversation Cards

Life Experience, Backup Plans, and Bouncing Back

Work in trios. Pick 2–3 questions per card and share real stories, not just opinions. Click any highlighted word to check its meaning.
CARD 1

Life Experiences That Shaped You

  • Think about a life experience that really changed how you see things. What happened, and how did it change you?
  • Were you brought up by someone who taught you an important lesson early in life? What was it?
  • Is there something you learned as a kid that took years to really sink in?
Key words
brought up by sink in
CARD 2

Backup Plans: When Things Go South

  • Do you usually make a backup plan in case something goes south, or do you prefer to deal with problems as they come?
  • Have you ever been part of a plan where the details just didn't add up — the timing, the numbers, or the whole idea?
  • If your current plan for the future fell apart tomorrow, what would your next move be?
Key words
go south didn't add up
CARD 3

The Role of Family Support

  • How important is it to have family you can call when things go wrong, even if you don't talk to them regularly?
  • Has your family ever helped you avoid getting caught in a con or a bad deal, just by giving you good advice?
  • Do you ever rant about a problem to a family member, just to feel better — even if you don't expect them to fix it?
Key words
regularly con rant about
CARD 4

Dealing with Setbacks

  • When you screw up at work or in life, how do you usually bounce back?
  • Have you ever wanted to yell out of pure frustration after something went wrong? What helped you calm down?
  • What keeps you motivated to keep at it, even when a setback makes you want to quit?
Key words
screw up yell keep at it
CARD 5

Looking Back, Moving Forward

  • Some people spend their whole lives trying to become well-off, while others just want stability. What matters more to you?
  • Has anyone ever given you a hint that a big change was coming, before you actually realized it yourself?
  • If you had to describe one humongous challenge you've overcome, what would it be — and what got you through it?
Key words
well-off hint humongous
