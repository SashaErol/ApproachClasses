Clothes & Supplies

Uniforms and School Supplies

Warm-up: Do you wear a uniform at school?
Fact: In the US, most public schools don't have uniforms. Students buy their own notebooks, pencils, and a backpack.
  1. Do students wear uniforms in your country?
  2. Do you like uniforms, or normal clothes, better?
  3. Do students buy their own school supplies, or does the school give them?
  4. Is it expensive to buy school supplies in your country?
Key words
backpack notebook
2
Food & Rules

Cafeteria Food and School Rules

Warm-up: What do you eat for lunch at school?
Fact: In the US, many schools have a cafeteria. Students can buy lunch or bring food from home. Schools also have rules, like "no phones in class."
  1. Does your school have a cafeteria?
  2. Do students bring lunch from home, or buy it at school?
  3. What are some rules at your school?
  4. Can students use their phones in class in your country?
Key words
lunch allowed
3
Time Off

School Breaks and Summer Vacation

Warm-up: How long is your summer vacation?
Fact: In the US, students have a long summer vacation, about two or three months. They also have short breaks, like winter break and spring break.
  1. How many school breaks do you have in one year?
  2. Is your summer vacation long or short?
  3. What do you usually do during summer vacation?
  4. Do you think a long summer vacation is good or bad? Why?
Key words
winter break spring break
4
Calendar & Teachers

Back to School and Teachers

Warm-up: When do students go back to school in your country?
Fact: In the US, school usually starts in August or September. Students often have a different teacher every year.
  1. When does school start in your country?
  2. Do you have the same teacher every year, or a new one?
  3. Do you like having a new teacher every year? Why or why not?
  4. Is the first day of school a big event in your country?
Key words
start every year
5
School Levels

Class Size and School Levels

Warm-up: How many students are in your class?
Fact: In the US, schools are usually separated into elementary school, middle school, and high school. A class usually has 20 to 30 students.
  1. How many kids are in a classroom in your country?
  2. Are elementary, middle, and high school in separate buildings in your country?
  3. Do you think small classes or big classes are better?
  4. What age do kids start elementary school in your country?
Key words
middle school high school
