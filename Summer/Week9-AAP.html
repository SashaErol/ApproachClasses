Right or Wrong?

A fast vocabulary game. You'll see a word and one possible meaning — decide if it's Right or Wrong before time runs out.

How to play:
• You have 25 seconds per word.
• Answer faster to earn more points — even correct answers earn less if you're slow.
• Wrong answers or running out of time earn 0 points.
• 15 words total. Good luck!
