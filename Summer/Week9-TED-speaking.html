Warm-Up Cards

Life Abroad, Growing Older, and Living in the Moment

Work in trios. Pick 2–3 questions per card and share real stories, not just opinions. Click any highlighted word to check its meaning.
CARD 1

Missing the Holiday Spirit Back Home

  • Is there a smell, a song, or a dish that instantly takes you back to a holiday from your childhood?
  • Do the holidays here in the US feel the same to you, or does something about them just feel off compared to back home?
  • What do you miss most about celebrating back home — the food, the noise, the whole atmosphere?
Key words
takes you back feel off
CARD 2

Raising Kids Far From Family

  • What's the hardest part about raising kids here without your parents or siblings around to help out?
  • Do you ever feel like you're figuring it all out on your own, without a safety net?
  • How do you deal with the lonely moments — the ones when you wish you could just drop the kids off at grandma's for a few hours?
Key word
safety net
CARD 3

Is There an Age Limit on Fun?

  • Do you think there's an age when you're just supposed to stop going to parties or dressing a certain way?
  • Have you ever felt judged for dyeing your hair a bright color or wearing something short or flashy, just because of your age?
  • Where do you personally draw the line when it comes to acting your age?
Key words
draw the line acting your age
CARD 4

Your Language, Music, and Stories

  • Have you noticed any surprising similarities between your language and English — maybe a word that sounds almost the same?
  • Do the stories or folk tales you grew up with share themes with American movies or books?
  • Is there a song in your language that just hits different compared to its English equivalent?
Key word
hits different
CARD 5

Living the Moment vs. Filming It

  • Do you ever catch yourself reaching for your phone instead of just taking in a beautiful moment with your own eyes?
  • Do you think posting something on social media right away kind of ruins the moment, or does it help you hold onto it?
  • Have you ever regretted spending too much time filming something instead of actually living it?
Key word
catch yourself
