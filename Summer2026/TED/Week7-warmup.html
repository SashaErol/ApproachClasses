Warm-Up Speaking Cards

Confidence, Feedback, and Finding Your Voice

Work in trios. Pick 2–3 questions from each card and share real stories, not just opinions. Click any highlighted word to check its meaning.
CARD 1

Where Does Confidence Come From?

  • Do you think confidence is something we're born with, or something we build over time through experience?
  • Have you noticed that traits like shyness or being outgoing feel almost natural — like they were part of your personality since childhood?
  • Can someone actually teach themselves to be more confident, or does it always come down to how they were raised?
CARD 2

Giving and Receiving Feedback

  • Do you find it easier to give feedback to someone, or to receive it yourself?
  • Tell us about a time someone gave you feedback that actually helped you improve. What made it work?
  • When you get criticized, does it stay in your head for a while, or do you move past it quickly?
CARD 3

Home and Confidence

  • Growing up, did anyone in your family ever say something — maybe without meaning to — that stuck with you and affected your confidence?
  • How much do you think your upbringing shaped how confident you feel today as an adult?
  • Is it possible to fully move past comments from childhood that created some real insecurity, or do they always stay with us a little?
Key words
upbringing insecurity
CARD 4

A Feedback Story From Work

  • Think of a boss or manager who gave you memorable feedback, good or bad. How did it affect you?
  • Has a mistake at work ever hurt your confidence for a while afterward?
  • What helped you find the grit to keep going after a rough moment on the job?
Key word
grit
CARD 5

Feedback at Approach

  • How do teachers at Approach usually give you feedback? Does it feel encouraging, or a little intimidating?
  • What do you personally expect from a teacher when they correct your English?
  • Do you think feedback should always come with some praise attached, or is direct correction more useful on its own?
CARD 6

Do We Need Validation From Others?

  • How important is it for you to get validation from classmates or coworkers when you do something well?
  • Do you ever feel like you need someone else's approval to actually feel good about your own work?
  • Can needing too much outside approval actually hold a person back?
Key word
validation
