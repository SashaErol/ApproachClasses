Guns

Does Everyone Have a Gun?

Warm-up: Do people have guns in your country?
Fact: The data says 1 in 3 Americans owns a gun.
  1. Do many people have a gun in your country? Is it common?
  2. Do you think a gun makes a home more or less safety?
  3. Is gun violence a problem in your country?
  4. If you could change one gun law, what would you change? Why?
Key words
own dangerous

City & Countryside

City or Countryside?

Warm-up: Do you feel safer in a big city or in the countryside?
Fact: In rural areas, people often use guns for hunting. In urban areas, guns feel more scary because of violence.
  1. Is a big city more dangerous than a small town in your country?
  2. Do people hunt in your country?
  3. What makes you feel unsafe in a city?
  4. How would you make your city feel safer?
Key words
countryside scary
3
Travel

Do Americans Travel Abroad?

Warm-up: Do you like to travel to other countries, or stay in your own country?
Fact: Many Americans don't have a passport. But they travel a lot inside the US, because the country is very big.
  1. Do you have a passport? Have you traveled to another country?
  2. Is it easy or difficult to travel internationally from your country?
  3. Why do you think many Americans don't travel internationally?
  4. Would you rather travel domestically or internationally? Why?
Key words
border convenient
4
Vacation

Vacation Time and Money

Warm-up: How many vacation days do you have every year?
Fact: Many American workers don't have a lot of vacation time or money. So they visit a beach close to home instead of another country.
  1. How many vacation days do people get every year in your country?
  2. Is it expensive to travel from your country to another country?
  3. Do you prefer a relaxing trip or a more hectic trip?
  4. How would you improve vacation time for workers in your country?
Key words
enjoyable hassle
5
Values

Are Americans Materialistic?

Warm-up: Do people care a lot about money and things in your country?
Fact: Some people think Americans are materialistic. But the data says this stereotype is only somewhat true.
  1. Are people in your country materialistic? Why or why not?
  2. Is it good or bad to want a lot of things?
  3. Do you think "Americans are materialistic" is a fair stereotype?
  4. If this stereotype is not fully true, how would you explain it better?
Key words
data somewhat
