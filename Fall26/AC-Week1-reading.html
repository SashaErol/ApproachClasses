Tips to Feel at Home

You are new in Massachusetts. You want new friends. Here are some tips.

Find a free speaking group. Approach ISC has a group every week. The library has one too. You can practice English and meet your community.

Talk to your neighbors. Say “Hi!” You can try small talk. Talk about the weather. You do not need big topics.

Be careful with parking. Some streets need a permit. Some streets say “No Parking” on snow days. Read the signs. If you don't, you can get a ticket.

Americans like personal space. Standing a little far away is OK. It is not rude.

Practice a little every day. Soon you will feel comfortable in your community.
