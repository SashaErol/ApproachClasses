Local Teams

Popular Teams in Massachusetts

Warm-up: What is a popular sports team in your country?
Fact: In Massachusetts, popular teams are the Patriots (football), the Red Sox (baseball), the Celtics (basketball), and the Bruins (hockey).
  1. What is your favorite sport?
  2. Do you have a favorite team?
  3. Is soccer popular in your country?
  4. Do people wear team shirts in your country?
Key words
hockey basketball
2
Where They Play

Stadiums and Arenas to Visit

Warm-up: Do you have a big stadium in your city?
Fact: The Patriots play at Gillette Stadium. The Red Sox play at Fenway Park. The Celtics and Bruins play at TD Garden, an indoor arena.
  1. What is the name of a stadium in your country?
  2. Have you ever watched a game inside a stadium?
  3. Would you like to visit Fenway Park one day?
  4. Do you prefer an outdoor stadium or an indoor arena?
Key words
indoor outdoor
3
Money

How Much Are the Tickets?

Warm-up: Is it expensive to watch a live game in your country?
Fact: A baseball ticket can cost about $50. A football ticket can cost $150 or more.
  1. How much does a game ticket usually cost in your country?
  2. Do you think American tickets are expensive?
  3. Do you prefer to watch a game at the stadium or on TV?
  4. Is it cheaper to watch a game at home?
Key words
cheap seat
4
Rules

Rules at the Stadium

Warm-up: Do stadiums in your country have special rules?
Fact: Many US stadiums only allow small, clear bags. You cannot bring outside food or drinks. There is a security check at the door.
  1. What rules do stadiums have in your country?
  2. Can you bring your own food to a game in your country?
  3. Do you think these rules are good or bad? Why?
  4. Is there a security check at games in your country?
Key words
allowed clear bag
5
Game Day

Game Day Traditions

Warm-up: What do fans do before a game in your country?
Fact: Many Americans tailgate before a game — they cook food in the parking lot. Fans wear team colors and cheer loudly.
  1. Do people cook food before a game in your country?
  2. Do fans wear their team's colors?
  3. What food do people eat at games in your country?
  4. Do you like a loud, exciting atmosphere at games?
Key words
cheer parking lot
